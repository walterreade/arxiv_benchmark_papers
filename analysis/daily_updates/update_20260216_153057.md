## When Words Don’t Mean What They Say: Figurative Understanding in Bengali Idioms

[https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.12921](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.12921)

**Date:** 2026-02-13

The benchmark measured the ability of Large Language Models to comprehend the figurative meaning of Bengali idioms. This included idioms with cultural, historical, and religious dimensions, where 'religious_significance' was an explicit annotation field in the dataset. The paper did not present specific findings related to religion. However, it established a dataset where idioms are annotated for 'religious_significance'. The general finding was that all tested LLMs performed poorly (no model surpassed 50% accuracy) in understanding Bengali idioms, highlighting a critical failure in comprehending deep cultural, and by extension religious, contexts embedded in figurative language.


## BaziQA-Benchmark: Evaluating Symbolic and Temporally Compositional Reasoning in Large Language Models

[https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.12889](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.12889)

**Date:** 2026-02-13

The benchmark measures the ability of large language models to perform symbolic and temporally compositional reasoning within the framework of Bazi (Eight Characters) astrology, a traditional East Asian symbolic system. It evaluates structured inference over a fixed symbolic chart and interacting temporal conditions. All evaluated models performed significantly above the 25% random chance baseline, demonstrating a non-trivial capability for symbolic reasoning within the Bazi system. However, their absolute accuracy remains far from saturation (highest was 36.7%), highlighting the difficulty of the task. Model performance showed pronounced sensitivity to temporal composition and reasoning order, and they exhibited systematic failures on tasks requiring precise temporal localization and multi-condition symbolic judgments.
