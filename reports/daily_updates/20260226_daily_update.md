## Do LLMs Adhere to Label Definitions? Examining Their Receptivity to External Label Definitions

[https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.02452](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.02452)

**Date:** 2026-02-24

Hate speech detection against protected groups, including on the basis of religion. The paper does not provide specific findings related to religion. The analysis of the HATEXPLAIN (hate speech) dataset, which includes religion as a protected category, is aggregated and not broken down by specific attributes. The general finding for this task was that model performance improves substantially with precise label definitions, suggesting a 'knowledge integration threshold' where models switch from internal representations to trusting external definitions.
